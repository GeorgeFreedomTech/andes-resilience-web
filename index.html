Body Awareness Training

Conscious Movement. Breathing. Relaxation.

The Practice

Slow, controlled movements performed standing, lying down or sitting. Training that, combined with conscious breathing and relaxation, will help you become more aware of your body and develop a better relationship with it.

No special equipment needed. Practice at home, at work or outdoors.

Your Body Is Your Home

Your body is the one place you will inhabit throughout your entire life. Get to know it, take care of it and learn to move better with it.
